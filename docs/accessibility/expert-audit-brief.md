# ELDERCARE 360: Expert Accessibility Audit Brief

## 1. Scope & Objective
This brief authorizes an independent external accessibility auditor (IAAP Certified Web Accessibility Specialist - WAS / CPACC) to conduct an exhaustive evaluation of ELDERCARE 360 against **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** standards, plus selected **Level AAA** criteria engineered for older adults.

---

## 2. In-Scope Targets & User Journeys
1. **Elder Interface (Simple Mode)**:
   - Day Ring & Today Medicine Schedule.
   - Hold-to-Confirm & 10-Minute Undo Toast.
   - Emergency Contact & "Call My Family" modal.
   - Comfort Profile panel (Text Size, Spacing, High Contrast, Read Screen Aloud).
2. **Family Dashboard**:
   - Prescription proposal and two-person approval dialog.
   - Adherence Evidence-Mix Bar and weekly report.
   - Plain-Language Access Ledger.
3. **Caregiver Task View**:
   - Task completion and handover notes.
4. **Authentication & Onboarding**:
   - Email/password, Google Sign-In, and numeric PIN keypad.

---

## 3. Assistive Technologies to Validate
- **Screen Readers**: NVDA (Firefox/Chrome), JAWS, Apple VoiceOver (iOS/macOS), Android TalkBack.
- **Switch Devices**: Single-switch and two-switch scanning.
- **Magnification Software**: ZoomText / Windows Magnifier at 400% zoom.
- **Speech Input**: Dragon NaturallySpeaking / Voice Control.
