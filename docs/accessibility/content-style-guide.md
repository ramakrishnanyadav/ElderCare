# ELDERCARE 360: Plain Language & Accessible Content Style Guide

## 1. Principles of Calm, Respectful Communication
1. **Dignity & Agency**: The elder is an autonomous adult, never spoken down to or treated as passive.
2. **Everyday Words Over Clinical Terms**:
   - Use **"Medicine"**, never *"Medication"* or *"Pharmaceutical agent"*.
   - Use **"Doctor visit"**, never *"Clinical encounter"* or *"Consultation"*.
   - Use **"Ask your family"**, never *"Trigger escalation protocol"*.
   - Use **"Who can see this"**, never *"Access control grant list"*.
3. **Sentence Length & Simplicity**:
   - Elder copy target: **Maximum 12 words per sentence**. One idea per sentence.
   - Flesch-Kincaid Reading Grade: $\le 6.0$ for Elder Mode; $\le 8.0$ for Family / Caregiver.

---

## 2. Action Verbs & Button Labels
Buttons must explicitly declare the physical or digital outcome:
- **CORRECT**: `"I took it"` | `"Not yet"` | `"Skip this one"` | `"Call my daughter"` | `"Undo this action"`
- **FORBIDDEN**: `"OK"` | `"Submit"` | `"Confirm"` | `"Execute"` | `"Dismiss"`

---

## 3. Human Time, Numbers & Units
- **Time Descriptions**:
  - Say `"8:00 in the morning"`, not `"08:00 hrs"`.
  - Say `"Tomorrow at 4:30 in the afternoon"`, not `"2026-11-16 16:30"`.
- **Numbers with Explicit Units**:
  - Always write `"5 mg"`, `"1 tablet"`, `"2 drops"`. Never bare numerals without context.
- **Evidence-Graded Acknowledgements**:
  - Never state *"Medicine was swallowed"*.
  - Output: *"You said you took it at 8:05 in the morning"* (`PATIENT_REPORTED`).

---

## 4. Three-Part Error Message Formula
Every error, toast, and warning must answer three specific questions in plain language:
1. **What happened**: *"We could not save your note right now."*
2. **What it means**: *"Your internet connection seems to have paused."*
3. **What to do next**: *"We saved your words on your phone and will send them when internet returns."*
