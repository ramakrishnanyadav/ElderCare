# Security & Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

## 1. Safe Harbor Policy (NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW)
We welcome security researchers and community members who identify potential vulnerabilities in ELDERCARE 360. If you act in good faith and adhere to this policy:
- We will not pursue legal action or report you to law enforcement for research activities conducted within scope.
- We will acknowledge your contribution in our security hall of fame (unless you request anonymity).
- We commit to triaging reports within 48 hours and resolving confirmed critical vulnerabilities within 7 days.

---

## 2. In-Scope & Reporting Instructions
Please report security issues directly to: `security@eldercare360.local` or via our security bug reporting endpoint.

**Required Information:**
- Detailed description of the potential vulnerability.
- Step-by-step reproduction instructions or proof-of-concept script.
- CVSS v3.1 estimation and potential clinical impact.

**Out of Scope:**
- Physical attacks against elder devices or clinical facilities.
- Volumetric Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks.
- Social engineering attacks against family members or caregivers.
